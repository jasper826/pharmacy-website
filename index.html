About Our Pharmacy

Serving the Arba Minch Community Since 2016

Established on November 21, 2016, Kana Drug Store was founded with a singular, vital mission: to ensure that no patient or medical facility in Arba Minch and the wider Gamo zone ever lacks access to critical, authentic, and life-saving pharmaceuticals.

Strategically located in front of Arba Minch General Hospital and just minutes from Arba Minch Referral Hospital, our pharmacy serves as an essential healthcare anchor. We operate 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, 365 days a year, ensuring that families confronting late-night emergencies and healthcare practitioners performing acute interventions always have an open door.

Our Dual Commitment:

  • For Individual Citizens (B2C): Compassionate counseling, rapid prescription fulfillment, affordable local pricing in ETB, and the guarantee that we stock "every kind of medicine" you or your loved ones require.
  • For Healthcare Institutions (B2B): Dedicated wholesale procurement for hospitals, private clinics, diagnostic centers, and NGOs. We offer bulk inventory stability, strict regulatory batch compliance, and scheduled deliveries.
